## Crispy mutton (goat) kheema spicy noodles
1 lb goat kheema batch; servings not specified
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- 1 lb goat kheema
- 1 large red onion, finely diced in a food processor
- ½ small cabbage, finely chopped in a food processor
- 1 pack wide rice noodles — pack weight not specified
- Haldi (turmeric)
- Hot chili powder
- Ground black pepper — generous amount
- Kashmiri chili powder
- Grated ginger
- Grated garlic
- Salt
- Cumin
- Worcestershire sauce
- 1–1½ tbsp Shaan kheema masala
- Light soy sauce
- Coconut aminos
- Rice vinegar
- Lao Gan Ma — fermented soybean version preferred
- Oil: 1 tsp, then 1–2 tsp, then 1 oil spoonful — spoon size not specified
- Water as needed; reserve 1 cup noodle cooking water

### Method
1. Put the kheema in a dry cooking pan. Add salt, a generous amount of black pepper, haldi, hot chili powder, Kashmiri chili powder, Worcestershire sauce, grated ginger and grated garlic. Mix and marinate for 15–20 minutes.
2. Bring water to a boil and soak the rice noodles.
3. Brown the kheema over high to medium-high heat until crispy. Transfer to a bowl.
4. Add 1 tsp oil to the pan, add the onion, salt it and sauté for 30 seconds, scraping up browned bits.
5. Add the cabbage and continue deglazing. Reduce the heat if the vegetables burn instead of releasing moisture.
6. Rinse the noodles, reserving 1 cup of noodle cooking water in the pot.
7. When the onion is translucent and cabbage has softened, push the vegetables to the side. Add 1–2 tsp oil to the center.
8. When hot, add cumin and toast for 30 seconds without burning. Add grated ginger and garlic and sauté for 5–10 seconds.
9. Add 1–1½ tbsp kheema masala and 1 oil spoonful of oil. Sauté briefly without burning.
10. Toss the vegetables with the masala. Add the rice noodles and reserved water. Mix well, add light soy sauce and mix again.
11. Add the cooked kheema and Lao Gan Ma. Mix, adding water if the noodles need more cooking or liquid is needed for a glaze.
12. Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Taste, add coconut aminos and salt, and cook until the water evaporates and the noodles are glazed.
13. Turn off the heat. Add a splash of rice vinegar and adjust seasonings.
Spices and sauces are to taste where no amount was supplied. Preserve goat kheema and the preferred fermented-soybean Lao Gan Ma. Contains soy; wheat depends on sauce brands.

Recipes on rotation

- RICE: 2 tbsp olive oil
- RICE: 1 tbsp Lao Gan Ma spicy chili crisp
- RICE: 1 tbsp soy sauce
- DRESSING: ½ cup Kewpie or regular mayonnaise
- DRESSING: 2 tbsp soy sauce

- SALAD: 1 cup edamame
- SALAD: 1 avocado, cubed

### Method

6. Add the rice and dressing to the salad and mix.
7. Top with sesame seeds and serve.

## Crispy pork chili oil noodles (Chef Fatty)
1 lb pork and 8 oz dried noodles; servings not specified
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground pork
- Garlic powder, for pork — optional annotation; amount not specified
- Chinese BBQ seasoning — optional annotation; amount not specified
- 1 tsp neutral oil
- Salt to taste
- Crisp-steamed broccoli — optional; quantity not specified
- Sliced oyster mushrooms — optional; quantity not specified
- SAUCE: 4 garlic cloves, minced (garlic powder was used instead)
- SAUCE: 1 tbsp soy sauce
- SAUCE: 1 tbsp chili oil
- SAUCE: 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- SAUCE: 1 tsp brown sugar
- SAUCE: ½ tsp sesame oil
- SAUCE: ¾ cup chicken stock
- 8 oz dried wide rice noodles
- Scallions, thinly sliced
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Extra chili oil, for drizzling

### Method
1. Soak the rice noodles in tap-hot water for 10 minutes while preparing the pork and sauce.
2. If using, mix the pork with Chinese BBQ seasoning and garlic powder. Heat the oil in a large, wide pan over medium-high heat. Spread pork in one layer and leave undisturbed for 2–3 minutes until the bottom caramelizes. Break it apart and cook 3–4 minutes more until crisp at the edges and cooked through. Drain excess fat if needed, leaving about 1 tbsp in the pan. Remove the pork.
3. Add the sauce garlic (or garlic powder), hoisin, soy sauce, chili oil, brown sugar, sesame oil and stock. Add oyster mushrooms if using. Bring to a simmer and reduce for 1–2 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened.
4. Add the drained noodles and toss for 2–3 minutes until they absorb the sauce and look dark and lacquered. Return the pork, add scallions and toss.
5. If using broccoli, put the salted, crisp-steamed broccoli in a large bowl. Pour the noodles and sauce over it and mix.
6. Finish with scallions, sesame seeds and a generous drizzle of chili oil.
Source credit supplied: Chef Fatty. Equipment: large pan. Parenthetical seasonings, garlic-powder substitution and vegetables preserve your annotations. Contains soy and sesame; wheat depends on sauces.

## Fried fish with curry leaves
½ kg fish batch; servings not specified
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- ½ kg fish, pieces or fillets
- 2 tbsp oil, or as needed
- FIRST MARINADE: 1 tbsp ginger–garlic paste or grated ginger and garlic
- FIRST MARINADE: Lemon juice as needed
- FIRST MARINADE: Salt as needed
- FIRST MARINADE: ¼ tsp turmeric powder
- FIRST MARINADE: ¾ tsp garam masala or another spice powder
- FIRST MARINADE: ½ tsp coriander powder, optional
- FIRST MARINADE: ½ tsp red chili powder or smoked paprika
- SECOND COATING: 1½ tbsp rice flour or all-purpose flour
- SECOND COATING: 1½ tbsp besan (gram flour), or substitute corn flour
- SECOND COATING: Salt as needed
- SECOND COATING: ¼–½ tsp red chili powder or paprika
- SECOND COATING: 1 sprig curry leaves, finely chopped, or other herbs
- Sliced onion and grated carrot, to garnish

### Method
1. Mix the first marinade ingredients into a paste. Taste and adjust before the marinade contacts raw fish.
2. Pat the fish dry; make slits in thick fillets such as salmon if needed. Coat with marinade, adding 1 tsp water if necessary to help it spread. Set aside.
3. Mix the second-coating ingredients. Coat both sides of the fish in this flour mixture.
4. Rest for 10–15 minutes until moisture from the fish dampens the flour and helps it adhere.
5. Heat a griddle, pan or tawa over low heat and spread with 2 tbsp oil. Pan-fry or shallow-fry until crusty on both sides and cooked through, flipping gently to keep the coating intact.
6. Garnish with sliced onion and grated carrot. Serve with a meal or as an appetizer.
7. Oven option, as supplied: after coating, brush generously with oil. Preheat the oven at 170°C for 15 minutes, then cook for about 25 minutes, adjusting for fish thickness and type.

## Roasted cauliflower and lentil salad
4–6 servings
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- 1 large red or yellow onion, halved and sliced
- 1 medium cauliflower (about 2 lb), cut into bite-size florets
- 5 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper
- 1 cup green or brown lentils
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 cups baby spinach (2 oz)
- Handful of fresh cilantro or mint leaves
- ½ cup sliced or slivered toasted almonds (2 oz)
- About 6 cups water, for cooking lentils

### Method
1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Put the onion and cauliflower on a large sheet pan. Toss with 4 tbsp olive oil, cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Spread in one layer and roast for 25–30 minutes, tossing halfway, until tender and caramelized.
3. Meanwhile, put the lentils and garlic in a large pot and cover with about 6 cups water. Salt generously and bring to a boil. Reduce to medium and cook uncovered for 15–20 minutes, until just tender. Drain; toss in the colander or mash the hot garlic with the back of a spoon to break it apart.
4. In a large serving bowl, stir together the yogurt, remaining 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt. Add the cauliflower, lentils, spinach and herbs. Toss and adjust salt and pepper.
5. Top with toasted almonds. Serve warm or at room temperature. Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
Contains dairy and almonds. Recipe source was not supplied.

## Taverna salad
4–6 servings
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- ⅓ cup plus 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp minced garlic (1 small clove)
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper
- 3 medium tomatoes, cored, seeded and diced into ½-inch pieces, or 1 cup halved cherry tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, rinsed
- 1 orange or yellow bell pepper, seeded and diced into ½-inch pieces
- ½ large English cucumber, seeded and diced into ½-inch pieces
- ½ cup pitted Kalamata olives
- ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
- ¼ cup minced red onion or shallot
- 2 tbsp drained capers, coarsely chopped
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 pita (6 inches)
- 1 block halloumi (8 oz), patted dry and sliced ¾ inch thick

### Method
1. Whisk ⅓ cup olive oil with the vinegar, garlic and oregano. Season with salt and pepper.
2. Combine the tomatoes, chickpeas, bell pepper, cucumber, olives, parsley, onion, capers and scallions in a large bowl. Toss with the dressing.
3. Cut the pita into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil and salt. Heat an 8-inch skillet over medium heat and toast the pita for about 5 minutes, tossing often, until golden. Transfer to a bowl and reserve the skillet.
4. Drizzle the halloumi with the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Heat the skillet over medium-high and cook the halloumi for 2–3 minutes per side, until golden. Transfer to a cutting board and cut into bite-size cubes.
5. Add the pita and halloumi to the salad, toss and serve.
Contains dairy and wheat. Chickpeas and halloumi retained as supplied. Recipe source was not supplied.

## Pankaj Bhadoria pav bhaji
4 servings · Preparation 20 minutes · Cooking 20 minutes (supplied)
Recipes on rotation

### Ingredients
- 5 medium potatoes, boiled
- 1 cup finely chopped tomatoes
- 1 cup finely chopped onions, plus remaining onion for serving
- ½ beetroot, boiled
- 1 green capsicum
- 1 cup cauliflower florets
- ¼ cup carrots
- ¼ cup beans
- ¼ cup shelled green peas
- ¼ cup garlic
- 10 whole Kashmiri red chilies
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 green chilies
- 3 tbsp pav bhaji masala
- Salt to taste
- 50 g butter, divided, with butter for toasting pav as needed
- 1 bunch fresh coriander leaves
- 12 pavs
- Turmeric for blanching cauliflower — amount not specified
- Lemon wedges, for serving
- Water: 1 cup for soaking chilies, ½ cup for garlic water, 1½ cups for bhaji

### Method
1. Deseed the Kashmiri chilies and soak in 1 cup boiling water. Grind into a paste; the grinding step makes the later chili-paste instruction explicit.
2. Grind the garlic with ½ cup water to make garlic water.
3. Peel and mash the boiled potatoes. Finely chop the onions, tomatoes, green chilies and capsicum. Mash the boiled beetroot with the vegetables; its use is made explicit because the supplied method does not name it again.
4. Blanch the cauliflower with salt and turmeric. Blanch the peas, carrots and beans, then drain and coarsely mash the vegetables.
5. Heat the oil and half the butter. Sauté the onion until light golden. Add pav bhaji masala and capsicum and sauté for 1 minute.
6. Add tomatoes and salt. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until oil separates.
7. Add mashed vegetables, potatoes, 2 tbsp red chili paste and 1½ cups water. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 15 minutes, mashing until smooth. Add 2–3 tbsp garlic water and cook 3–4 minutes more.
8. In a thick-bottomed pan or tawa, heat 1 tbsp butter with 1 tbsp chili paste. Once the chili colors the butter, add 3 ladles bhaji and cook 2–3 minutes.
9. Split the pavs horizontally and butter the cut sides. Toast on the tawa for about 30 seconds, pressing 2–3 times, until crisp and light brown.
10. Garnish the bhaji with coriander and remaining butter. Serve with pav, chopped onion and lemon wedges.
Source credit supplied: Pankaj Bhadoria. The listed 20-minute cooking time is retained, although the individual steps may take longer. Total butter allocation and when to add chopped green chilies were not fully specified. Contains dairy and wheat.
